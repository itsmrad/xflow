title: Introduction

---

xflow is voice dictation for developers. A small Rust daemon, `xflowd`, records while you hold a key, sends the audio to the speech provider you chose, cleans up the text and types it where your cursor is. There is no app window to switch to.

It runs on macOS and on Linux (GNOME, beta). It has a full CLI and a terminal UI, keeps your history in a local file, and sends no telemetry.

<Callout type="note">
xflow 0.1.0 is a development build. Desktop delivery and provider quality still need real-world acceptance on your machine. Run `xflow doctor` if something looks off.
</Callout>

## The 60-second tour

<Steps>
<Step title="Hold Fn and talk">
Hold <Kbd>Fn</Kbd> in any app — editor, terminal, browser, Slack. A small pill shows you are recording. Double-tap <Kbd>Fn</Kbd> to keep recording without holding the key.
</Step>
<Step title="Let go">
xflow transcribes, removes filler words, applies your dictionary and pastes the text at your cursor. If the field can't take it, the text stays on your clipboard and `xflow last` prints it.
</Step>
<Step title="Script it">
Everything the daemon does is a command. Pipe a dictation into another program, transcribe a file, or follow events as JSON.
</Step>
</Steps>

```sh
xflow listen --once --seconds 5   # print text, don't type it
xflow transcribe interview.wav    # files work too
xflow history search postgres --json
xflow watch --json | jq .state    # live events
```

## How it fits together

| Piece | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `xflowd` | The background daemon. Owns the microphone, the shortcut, provider calls and text delivery. |
| `xflow` | The CLI. Plain `xflow` in a terminal opens setup until it is done, then the control centre (`xflow tui`). |
| Speech provider | Groq, OpenAI, Deepgram and seven more, or a local server. You bring the key. |
| Cleanup (optional) | A chat model that fixes punctuation and filler words. If it fails, you get the original transcript. |
