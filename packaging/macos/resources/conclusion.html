xflow is installed and running

Open Terminal and type: xflow

The first time, it walks you through setup: your speech service and key, your microphone, then the Mac permissions (it opens System Settings at each one; find xflowd in the list and switch it on). It ends with a test: hold Fn and say something.

Use it: hold Fn to dictate. Double-tap Fn for hands-free, then press Fn again to stop.
Settings or check-up later: type xflow again.
Uninstall: xflow service uninstall && sudo rm /usr/local/bin/xflow /usr/local/bin/xflowd
